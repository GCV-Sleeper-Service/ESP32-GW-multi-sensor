- **LESSON-OPS-128:** Verify configuration equivalence before theorizing about measurement discrepancies. The C3's low watermark reading (636 B) was entirely explained by the missing override, not by architectural differences between RISC-V and Xtensa.

### 5. Context that carries forward to Phase VX / Phase 7

- The C3 has been running on stock 4 KB httpd stack throughout Phase V without crashing — this is because the C3's history responses are small enough that the httpd handler never overflows 4 KB. But it has zero margin, and any future handler change could trigger a crash.
