## External Reviewer Workflow

HIGH-RISK step. Send to ALL reviewers. Focus areas:

1. **Boot safety:** Can `restore_device_history_v2_()` crash on corrupted NVS data? What if `nvs_get_blob` returns wrong size? What if `meta.valid_segments` is larger than `max_slots`?
2. **Budget correctness:** Does the math produce reasonable slot counts? For 640 KB partition with 4 persistent devices (3 high + 1 low), expected ~355 slots/high device, ~151 slots/low.
3. **Metric mapping:** Is the persist_idx→metric_states mapping consistent between persist (v7.7.1.3) and restore (this step)?
4. **Memory safety:** `DeviceSegment` (226 B) on stack in restore loop — safe? `RetentionBudget[NUM_DEVICES]` on stack — safe for NUM_DEVICES=5?
5. **Hash collision:** What happens if two device IDs hash to the same FNV-1a value? Is the collision detection in restore sufficient?
6. **Power loss:** If power is lost during budget recalculation + meta save, is the state recoverable on next boot?
