### 4. New lessons or critical rules?

**Candidate LESSON-OPS addition (assign final number at next lessons update):**

> **`bump-version.sh` must call `bundle-dashboard.sh --write` before `render_sensor_config.py
> --write`.** Without this, a version bump regenerates generated markers into `dashboard.js` and
> the bundle step would then overwrite them. Any script that invokes the regeneration pipeline must
> invoke it in full canonical order: bundle → generator → fixtures → minify → header → check.

**Critical Rule 37 — wording clarification required:**

The current wording of Critical Rule 37 in `prompts/prompt-index-and-workflow.md` shows:

> _"Full pipeline: `render_sensor_config.py --write` → `generate-fixtures.js` → `minify-dashboard.sh`
> → `generate-header.sh` → `render_sensor_config.py --check`"_

This predates v7.6.5.1 and omits `bundle-dashboard.sh --write` as Step 1. The rule wording must
be updated to the canonical 6-step form established in LESSON-OPS-091 at v7.6.5.1:

1. `bash scripts/bundle-dashboard.sh --write`
2. `python3 scripts/render_sensor_config.py --write`
3. `node tests/fixtures/generate-fixtures.js`
4. `bash scripts/minify-dashboard.sh`
5. `bash scripts/generate-header.sh`
6. `python3 scripts/render_sensor_config.py --check`
