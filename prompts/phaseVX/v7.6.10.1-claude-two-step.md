### Critical invariants

- All new board profiles MUST include the `external_components` block (Critical Rule 42 / BUG-083)
- All new partition tables MUST have `ota_0` at 0x10000 (BUG-061)
- C5 profile MUST include `CONFIG_XTAL_FREQ_48: 'y'` (48 MHz crystal, not 26 MHz)
- C5 partition table: 8 MB layout (not 4 MB, not 16 MB)
- S3 SuperMini and C6 SuperMini: 4 MB partition layout (same as C3/WROOM)
- Existing board profiles and partition tables are UNCHANGED

  • `ota_0` at 0x10000 in all 3? (BUG-061)
